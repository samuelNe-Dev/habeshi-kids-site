Privacy Policy — Habeshi Kids Studio

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Habeshi Kids Studio ("the app") is a local production tool used by the Habeshi Kids creator to send finished videos to their own TikTok account's inbox.

What the app accesses

  • TikTok account basics (scope user.info.basic): the account's display name and open ID, only to confirm that videos go to the correct account.
  • Video upload (scope video.upload): to place a finished video in the account's TikTok inbox.

How data is stored

Access and refresh tokens are stored only on the creator's own computer, in a local file that is never shared or uploaded anywhere except to TikTok's own API when making requests. The app has no server and no database.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect data about viewers or other TikTok users.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not post publicly on its own; the account owner posts from the TikTok app.
  • It is not directed at children as users. Habeshi Kids videos are made for families; the app itself is used only by the adult creator.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions, which ends the app's ability to upload.
